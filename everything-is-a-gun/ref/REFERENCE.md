This mod concept transforms standard _Minecraft_ survival gameplay into a weaponized shooter experience. Here are the core mechanics inspired by SSundee's new video:

- **Universal Block Conversion:** Every item and block in the game is capable of being transformed into a unique weapon (e.g., _fence posts_, _chests_, _raw gold_, or _wither skeleton skulls_). These items are assigned specific gun-like characteristics, such as damage output, firing rate, and ammo capacity (0:01-0:30).
- **Weapon Statistics Interface:** When hovering over an item/weapon in the inventory, an interface displays detailed stats, including **damage**, **attack rate**, **accuracy**, **recoil**, and **magazine size**. Some weapons support **akimbo** (dual-wielding) mechanics for increased combat efficiency (1:10-1:20).
- **Shooting-Based Mining:** A key mechanic allows players to "mine" blocks by shooting them with specific weapons. The mined materials are then automatically collected into the player's inventory (4:25-4:55).
- **Mob Combat AI:** Mobs are updated to also wield these custom guns, making survival scenarios significantly more dangerous. Mobs have their own combat behavior, ranging from pistols to grenade launchers (5:15-5:45).
- **Advanced Combat Features:** The mod includes mechanics like **scope zooming** (13:07-13:12), **universal enemy revealing** (2:52-3:00), and player **teleportation** to tracked enemies (2:59-3:05).
- **Boss Scaling:** The boss encounters (specifically the Wither) are evolved to utilize complex weaponry, such as **ray guns** and **sniper-like arms**, requiring players to constantly acquire higher-tier resources like _diamond_ or _Nether Star_ based weapons to compete (19:50-20:50).
